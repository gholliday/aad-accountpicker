# Privacy

This extension requests the following permissions:

## Permissions

- **identity / identity.email** — Used to read your Edge profile email address. This is matched against account tiles on Microsoft login pages to auto-select the correct account. Your email is never transmitted anywhere; it is only used locally for matching.

- **storage** — Used to save your preferences (delay settings) and track how many clicks the extension has saved you. Data is stored locally in your browser.

## Host Permissions

The extension only runs on the specific login pages that are listed in the manifest.

## Data Collection

This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All processing happens locally in your browser.
